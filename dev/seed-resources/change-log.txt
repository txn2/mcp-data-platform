Inventory reporting change log

2026-08-14  Weeks-of-supply now excludes stores open under 8 weeks rather than
            annualizing their short history. Affects W-1043 and C-3410 only.
2026-07-02  Shrink moved from an estimate to counted-only. Prior periods are
            not restated; the series has a level shift at this date.
2026-05-20  Comparable store sales excludes a store for both periods when it
            was closed for any part of either. Previously it was excluded from
            the closed period alone, which inflated the comp.
2026-03-11  Bayside Market (W-1042) enters the comp base.
