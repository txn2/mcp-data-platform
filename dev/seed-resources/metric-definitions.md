# Metric Definitions

**Weeks of supply.** On-hand units divided by the trailing 8-week average of
weekly units sold, at store-SKU grain. Stores open less than 8 weeks are
excluded rather than annualized.

**Sell-through.** Units sold divided by units received, over the life of a
receipt. It is not a rate per week; comparing sell-through across SKUs with
different receipt dates is meaningless.

**Shrink.** The difference between book inventory and counted inventory,
valued at cost. Recorded only at a physical count, so it is a step function,
not a trend.

**Comparable store sales.** Sales at stores open for the whole of both the
current and prior period. A store that closed for remodeling is out of the
comparison for both periods, not just the one it was closed in.
